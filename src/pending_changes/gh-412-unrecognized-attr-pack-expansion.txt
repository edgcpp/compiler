[GH #412]
Unrecognized attribute with a pack expansion in its arguments

In a variadic template, an unrecognized attribute whose arguments referenced a
parameter pack (e.g., in a pack expansion) was treated as a pack expansion of
the attribute as a whole.  As a result, the C++-generating back end could put
out a spurious "..." after the attribute when generating a template from its
IL, and instantiations could receive the wrong number of copies of the
attribute (e.g., one per pack element, or none for an empty pack).  For
example, in Clang mode:

  template <class... M> struct scoped_lock {
    scoped_lock(M&... m) __attribute__((acquire_capability(m...))) {}
  };

could be generated as

  scoped_lock(M &...m) __attribute((acquire_capability(m ... )...)) { }

That is now fixed.
